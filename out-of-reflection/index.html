A LITTLE GUIDE TO DOUBLE TAKES

How to play

  1. Compare the two views. Mirror reversal is normal.
  2. Select a mismatch in either view. After each correct find, the remaining differences change. Compare again before your next click.
  3. Find 3 differences in the living room, 5 in the kitchen and 7 in the bedroom. Complete each room, then select Continue to enter the next.
LOOK BEFORE YOU CLICK.

You can make two mistakes in each room. A third wrong selection means retrying that room. Completed rooms stay complete. Hints are free.

Keyboard: Tab to an object, Enter or Space to select it.
