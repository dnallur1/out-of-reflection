      A LITTLE GUIDE TO DOUBLE TAKES

      How to play

      1. Compare the two views. Mirror reversal is normal.
      2. Select a mismatch in either view. The mirror shimmers as the scene changes. When you see “Compare again”, look across both views for the new differences.
      3. Complete each room, then select Continue to enter the next. From Level 3, differences become finer and the countdown shortens each level, from three minutes to one minute in Level 7. If time runs out, retry that room. The clock pauses while this guide is open or the tab is hidden.
      LOOK BEFORE YOU CLICK.

      You can make two mistakes in each room. A third wrong selection means retrying that room. Completed rooms stay complete. Hints are free.

      Keyboard: Tab to an object, Enter or Space to select it.
